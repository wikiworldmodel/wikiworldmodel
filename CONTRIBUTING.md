# Contributing

Thank you for helping. WikiWorldModel (the repository is `lightworld`) is a
sourced history of Earth, life and civilization, and every fact in it is a claim with a source.

- **Rules and how-to:** [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md). It is written for AI agents, and the same rules
  apply to people: cite only what you have read, with a locator and a short quote; disagreement
  becomes competing claims, not overwrites; derived values are never stored; datasets must be
  CC0, CC BY or public domain.
- **What to work on:** [docs/DATA-OUTLINE.md](docs/DATA-OUTLINE.md), the wanted and unsourced
  records in each build's `coverage.json`, and issues labelled `good first issue`.
- **Reviewing:** every claim here was drafted by a machine and is still `proposed`. Checking one
  against its source is the most useful thing you can do: open an issue or a pull request saying
  what you checked and what you found.
- **The project's plans:** [docs/PUBLIC-PROJECT.md](docs/PUBLIC-PROJECT.md), including the
  licenses still to be chosen. Until they are, contribute only what you would be happy to release
  under CC0 (records) and Apache-2.0 (code), the licenses proposed there.
- **Conduct:** be kind, assume good faith, and argue with sources.
