# Archive

Documents whose work is done. They are kept for the decisions they record, and are no longer kept
up to date: their links into the code, their counts and their statuses are as they were when they
were last used, and some of what they say has since changed. Don't follow them as instructions;
the current rules are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md) and the current design in
[WORLD-MODEL.md](../WORLD-MODEL.md).

| Document | What it was |
|---|---|
| [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) | the plan that led to the sixth and seventh slices and the twenty record sets. The slices are built, and where each set stands is in [RECORD-SETS.md](../RECORD-SETS.md) |
| [examples/](examples/README.md) | about a hundred records written by hand, before the first slice, to find where the format was underspecified, with the gaps they found ([GAPS.md](examples/GAPS.md)) and their checker. The format has since settled differently in places, so they are not a model for new records: [RECORDS.md](../RECORDS.md) is |
