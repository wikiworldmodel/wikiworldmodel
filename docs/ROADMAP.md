# Roadmap: life at every place and time, and twenty record sets

Status: a plan, November 2026. It is written to be handed to an agent run where the datasets'
hosts can be reached; every source it names is a candidate until that run has read it, checked
its license and pinned it (see [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md)).

## The aim

Zoom into any place at any time and see what lived there, the way Rome in 315 CE appears when
you zoom into the city: the ground, the plants and the animals of that place and moment,
generated by the viewer from what the world records, and honest about which is which.

A living scene is made the way the city is:

- **Records set the constraints.** Which taxa lived there and then comes from fossil
  occurrences and the ranges the build derives from them; the biome from a field; the climate,
  coast and terrain from what the globe already draws.
- **A generator fills in the rest,** as the city's fabric fills in houses between the
  monuments: where each plant and animal stands, how many, what it is doing. It is seeded by
  place and time, so the build and every browser make the same scene.
- **Everything says what it rests on.** Click an animal and the viewer names its taxon, the
  occurrences that put it in this place and time, and what the generator invented. Evidence
  mode shows generated life in the same colour as generated housing.
- **Start small:** one place, one moment, a handful of taxa, done properly; then widen.

## Now: three steps

Built: see [SIXTH-SLICE.md](SIXTH-SLICE.md), which says what each criterion's test is and what fell
short. The lithologic indicators were not imported, and the scene follows the climate field's reading
of the place, which fossil leaves disagree with.

### 1. Biomes through time

What the land and shallow seas were covered with, as a gridded field by stage, from the
Cambrian to today. The globe paints land from it instead of from elevation alone, which ends a
limitation the second slice lists: land drawn green before there were land plants.

- **Records:** the transitions that bound it, each sourced: microbial crusts on land, the first
  land plants, the first forests, the spread of grasslands; reef builders through time.
- **Data to find and check:** lithologic indicators of climate (coals, evaporites, tillites,
  bauxites) by stage, such as the compilation of Boucot, Xu & Scotese (2013); climate belts or
  Köppen zones from the PALEOMAP paleoclimate work; stage-by-stage climate-model output (for
  example the HadCM3 Phanerozoic simulations of Valdes et al. 2021). Licenses unknown: a closed
  one may be cited and checked against, not imported.
- **Generator:** surface texture within each biome (forest, steppe, desert, ice, reef),
  procedural and marked so.
- **Done when:** no vegetation before the land-plants claim; biome boundaries move with the
  plates; the outline's check holds, that no forest fossil falls in a reconstructed desert.

### 2. A tree-of-life panel

A panel beside the globe showing the tree of life at the time on the slider: lineages branch
at their start, end at their extinction, and selecting a group lights up its fossils on the
globe and the timeline.

- **Data:** the topology already comes from the Open Tree of Life; times from the solver's
  bounds on each taxon. Diversity as line width waits for step 3's PBDB snapshot.
- **Done when:** the panel and the globe agree at every time; a taxon's bar starts within the
  solver's bounds; it works with the 26 taxa now, and with thousands.

### 3. A first living scene: the Hell Creek, about 66.5 Ma

The last ecosystem before the Chicxulub impact, which the world already records: the Hell
Creek Formation of Montana and the Dakotas, among the best-sampled dinosaur faunas and floras.

- **Data:** a dated snapshot of the Paleobiology Database (CC BY) for the formation: its
  occurrences, taxa and collections, with the query that made it, kept in `world/snapshots/`;
  body sizes and postures from the literature, as claims on the taxa.
- **Generator:** body-plan kits (ceratopsian, tyrannosaur, hadrosaur, crocodilian, turtle, small
  mammal; conifer, palm, broadleaf angiosperm, fern), sized from the claims, placed by the
  biome and a seeded layout, the way the city kit builds monuments from form claims.
- **Done when:** every organism in the scene is a taxon with an occurrence in the formation;
  nothing appears there that the records place elsewhere or elsewhen; clicking any of them says
  what is recorded and what was generated; the scene is the same to the byte in the build and
  the browser.

## Then: twenty record sets

In roughly this order, for breadth across time, life and civilization. Each is one or a few
bounded pull requests. The three steps above come out of sets 1–3.

Where each stands, what it added and what a reviewer should look at first is in
[RECORD-SETS.md](RECORD-SETS.md). All of it is proposed: nothing has been reviewed by a person.
